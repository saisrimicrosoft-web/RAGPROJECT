Company Policy Document

1. Work from Home Policy
Employees are allowed to work from home for up to two days a week. The standard work-from-home days are Tuesday and Thursday. Employees must ensure they have a stable internet connection and be available during core hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM).

2. Paid Time Off (PTO)
Each full-time employee is entitled to 20 days of paid time off per year. PTO requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Unused PTO does not roll over to the next year.

3. Office Dress Code
The office dress code is business casual. On Fridays, employees are allowed to wear casual clothing, provided it is neat and professional.

4. Expense Reimbursement
All business-related expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Approval from a direct manager is required before submitting the expense report.

5. Health and Safety
Employees must report any workplace hazards immediately to the HR department. Emergency exits are located at the front and back of the building.

6. Code of Conduct
We are committed to a respectful and inclusive workplace. Any form of harassment or discrimination will not be tolerated and may result in immediate termination.
